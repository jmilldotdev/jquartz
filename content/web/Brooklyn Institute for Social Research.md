The Brooklyn Institute for Social Research is an independent, community-based center for critical study that offers courses, programs, and events focused on philosophy, political theory, and social science. It provides opportunities for public engagement with complex intellectual topics through seminars, a podcast, and a certificate program, aiming to bridge the gap between academic research and the broader public.
