Noise is a platform that helps brands achieve growth by connecting them with an army of pay-as-you-go UGC creators. Businesses set their own price-per-view and monthly budget to generate social media content, such as video testimonials, demos, and slideshows, without the need for long-term contracts or upfront payments. The platform provides tools for creating content playbooks, managing brand guidelines, and tracking performance through realtime analytics.
