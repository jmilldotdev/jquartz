Weirdosphere is an online learning platform from Weird Studies hosts J.F. Martel and Phil Ford. It offers courses for artists, thinkers, and seekers drawn to the strange, the beautiful, and the esoteric. Through live lectures, deep readings, and community discussion, students explore ideas that challenge ordinary thought and awaken the imagination.
