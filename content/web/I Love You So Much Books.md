I Love You So Much Books (ILYSM)  is a community-centered bookstore committed to nurturing a safe space for discovery, curiosity and connection. Founded by Mariela Gavino in 2023 as a park-side pop-up offering a curation of used  books, ILYSM has been a vendor at various events in Richmond, Washington DC, and New York City. In February 2025, ILYSM’s Brookland Park Blvd shop opened on Valentine’s Day. We can’t wait to meet you! <3
