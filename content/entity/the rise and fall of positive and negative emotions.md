Negative emotions like fear, jealousy, anger, etc build quickly and fall slowly. 

Positive emotions like trust, love, comfort etc build slowly and fall quickly in general.

Social media algorithms are trying to engage you, and so they pick up on emotions which rise quickly. This may be an explanation for why social media algorithms seem to associate their objective with negativity.
