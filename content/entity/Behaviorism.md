> Reducing the number of variables in getting a subject to do what you want.

Consequences:

1. Abstract stimuli (Pavlov's dogs, gambling, the pictures of candy in Candy Crush)
2. Reward-Punishment Schemes are more effective when they have a slight element of randomness
