**Vibe Retrieval** is being able to retrieve reading highlights based on the current mood of what you're writing. and this is a more challenging thing to do than simple information retrieval since keywords and clustering don't do the job

Think of *finding an example of a vivid metaphor* - this is something where i have things saved that are appropriate, it is a factual question, but how to search for it is not immediately obvious using simple keyworded search terms.

Also related is GPT Moodboard, and saving the raw outputs of GPT-3 completions to use as source material for writings later. If there is tons of raw output, how do you search for it?
