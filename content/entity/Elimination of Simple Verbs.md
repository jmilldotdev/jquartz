> Instead of being a single word, such as *break, stop, spoil, mend, kill*, a verb becomes a phrase, made up of a **noun or adjective tacked on to some general-purposes verb** such as *prove, serve, form, play, render*.

> Characteristic phrases are: *render inoperative, militate against, prove unacceptable, make contact with, be subject to, give rise to, give grounds for, have the effect of, play a leading part (role) in, make itself felt, take effect, exhibit a tendency to, serve the purpose of*, etc. etc.
