> "Worrying is like paying a debt you don't owe"

An object that is made to be handled when you're anxious, with the physical feelings of touching the Worry Coin made to connect to the idea that worry and anxiety are often your own choice, which is sometimes a hard thing to remember in the grips of worry. It is a physical cue connected to a mental process.
